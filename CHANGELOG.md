# What's New

Each version's section is shown on the app's About page and used as its GitHub release notes, so
add one before running `Packaging/release.sh`. The first paragraph is the summary the sidebar
shows after updating.

## 1.2.0 — 12 November 2026

Easier to install, on Intel Macs too, and credits for the people behind the built-in wallpapers.

- **Disk image**: download MacPaperEngine.dmg, open it and drag the app onto Applications.
- **One-line install**: or paste a single command into Terminal, which installs MacPaperEngine and opens it, with no security prompt.
- **Intel Macs**: MacPaperEngine now runs on Intel Macs as well as Apple silicon.
- **Credits**: the abstract videos are by Colin Jones on Pexels, and the dynamic wallpapers come from Dynamic Wallpaper Club. Each is credited on its tab, in its tooltip and on the About page.
- **Find more**: a new tile on the Dynamic tab leads to Dynamic Wallpaper Club for more.

## 1.1.0 — 10 November 2026

The first public release.

- **Three kinds of wallpaper**: videos and GIFs, dynamic wallpapers that follow the sun or Light and Dark mode, and still pictures, each with its own tab.
- **Built-ins**: six abstract video loops and five dynamic wallpapers, downloaded the first time you pick one, so the app stays small.
- **Online**: browse Wallhaven for pictures and Pexels for videos, and set one in a click.
- **Play on Lock Screen**: your wallpaper on the Lock Screen too, with your previous one restored when you turn it off.
- **Hide Loop Jump**: your own videos restart without a visible jump.
- **Updates**: MacPaperEngine keeps itself up to date. Choose Automatic, Ask First or Manual in General > Updates.
- **Mac menus and shortcuts**: a real sidebar and toolbar, ⌘1 to ⌘9 for pages, ⌥⌘P to pause, ⌥⌘← and ⌥⌘→ to step through wallpapers.
